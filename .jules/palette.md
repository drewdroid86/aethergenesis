## 2025-05-15 - [Title] Pulsing Live Telemetry Indicator
**Learning:** Adding a subtle pulsing visual cue (e.g., a small emerald dot with `animate-pulse`) to information-dense panels significantly enhances the "live" feel of a data-driven simulation without adding cognitive load. It serves as a non-textual status indicator that the system is active.
**Action:** Use `animate-pulse` on small decorative status indicators in telemetry-heavy UIs to signify active data streaming. Pair with `aria-hidden="true"` for the decorative element and `aria-live="polite"` on the container to maintain accessibility.
